My method, from tag to deck plan

  1. Battle logs from one player tag

    Every recent battle on their main and second accounts, ranked and in events.

  2. Sort decks by game slot

    Their decks split into Game 1, 2 and 3, after a win and after a loss.

  3. Plan the remaining decks

    After each game I mark the burned cards and the decks still open to both players.

    g1 = log[log.game == 1]
    top = g1.deck.value_counts()
    # must match the raw log
    assert top.sum() == len(g1)
    return top.head(3)
